Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy is written for users in the United States. It takes into account the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and, to the extent they apply to Estimea, the consumer privacy laws of California (CCPA, as amended by the CPRA) and of other states (section 5). As the publisher is established in France, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the French Data Protection Act (loi Informatique et Libertés) also apply to the processing of your data.

1. DATA CONTROLLER

Thomas Lirola, sole proprietor established in France, publisher of Estimea (full details in the legal notice). For any question about your data, to exercise your rights or to ask for help, write to contactdexdevapp@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

2. WHAT WE PROCESS, WHY, AND ON WHAT BASIS

  • Photos and details you write: sent to the analysis service to identify the item, assess its condition, read useful markings (serial number, hallmark) and estimate it. Basis: performance of the service you request.
  • Account: identifier, email address and name provided by your Google account, through which you must sign in. Basis: performance of the service.
  • Estimates and inventory: items, prices, dates, categories, notes, and the sale price if you enter it. Basis: performance of the service.
  • Questions to the expert and negotiation coach: your questions and the details of the listing you enter are sent to the analysis service to write the answer. Basis: performance of the service.
  • Purchases and credits: paid exclusively through Google Play; Estimea never receives your payment card details. Each purchase is linked to your account by an encrypted fingerprint, so that what you are entitled to follows you to a new phone. Basis: performance of the contract and our accounting obligations.
  • Rewarded videos: Google AdMob confirms to us that a video has been watched (identifier of your account and of the transaction, date), to unlock a free estimate. Basis: performance of the service.
  • Link sharing: when you share an estimate page, it is published with your first name and, if you leave them, up to three photos with their GPS location removed; the serial number and the sale price never appear on it. Anyone who has the link can see it. Basis: your request.
  • Referral: your code, and the link between referrer and referred user, to pay the promised credits. Basis: performance of the service.
  • Reports: the reason, your comment, the estimate, answer or shared page reported (its link) and your account identifier, to correct the service or stop a link. Basis: our legitimate interest in the quality of the service.
  • Audience measurement: Firebase Analytics records how the app is used (screens opened, actions), without your account identifier or your device's advertising identifier. Basis: our legitimate interest in improving the app. You can object to it by writing to contactdexdevapp@gmail.com.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics receives a technical report when the app crashes (device model, version, error trace, chosen country and language). Basis: our legitimate interest in repairing the app. You can object to it by writing to contactdexdevapp@gmail.com.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob shows ads to free accounts. Google may personalize them using your device's advertising identifier and what it knows of your activity in other apps and on other websites, information it may collect over time and across services. We do not sell your personal information for money. Some state privacy laws may nevertheless treat this use of your data for advertising as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, or as “targeted advertising”: you can opt out of it at any time (section 5), and the ads then become non-personalized. Your choice changes nothing about what you receive in the app. The subscription and the 12-month pass remove all advertising. Basis: our legitimate interest in funding the free version, to which you can object at any time.
  • Children: Estimea is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506, and its rule, 16 CFR Part 312). If you are under 13, do not use Estimea. A parent who believes that their child under 13 has given us personal information can write to contactdexdevapp@gmail.com: we will delete it. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16. Where your state's law requires it (Texas), the app asks Google Play for your age range only to apply the age rule of the terms of use: the answer stays on your phone, is kept there for one day, and is never sent to our servers nor used for advertising or measurement.
  • In the categories used by California law, this is: identifiers (account identifier, email address, name, advertising identifier), commercial information (purchases and credits), internet or other electronic network activity (use of the app, crash reports), visual information (your photos), and what you write in the app. It comes from you, from your phone and from Google (sign-in, Google Play, AdMob). We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

3. WHO PROCESSES YOUR DATA, AND WHERE

Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and on the servers of Google, our only provider. Google processes it on our behalf, except for advertising and for Google Play payments, which Google also processes on its own account, under its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd): sign-in, database, photo storage, audience measurement and crash reports. The database and photos are hosted in the European Union.
  • Gemini API (Google): analysis of the photos and writing of the estimates and of the expert's and coach's answers. Google does not use this data to improve its products; it keeps requests and responses for a limited period, solely to detect abuse. When an estimate relies on a Google search, the query is kept for thirty days to produce that result.
  • Google AdMob: advertising and rewarded videos. Google Play: payments.
  • Where your data goes: it is stored outside the United States, in the European Union, where the database and photos are hosted, and the publisher is established in France; the functions that receive your photos and produce the estimates run in the United States, at Google LLC, which participates in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for the data protected by the GDPR.

4. HOW LONG

  • On your phone: as long as the app is installed. Your photos stay there, unless you turn on their deletion after analysis.
  • Your account and your synced estimates, as well as your photos if you have turned on “Back up my photos too”: until you delete them, or delete your account.
  • The copy of each result produced by the server, used for link sharing: 180 days.
  • A shared estimate page: 90 days, or less if you stop sharing it.
  • The confirmation of a watched video: 90 days after it has been used.
  • Reports: until your account is deleted.
  • The purchase register (product, date, technical account identifier, without name or address): kept after the account is deleted, so that the same receipt cannot be used twice, and for accounting.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

As the publisher is established in France, Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR give you the rights below, wherever you live. The privacy laws of California (Civil Code, sections 1798.100 and following) and of a growing number of other states, such as Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and Virginia, give similar rights; most of them apply only to businesses above certain thresholds — in California, for example, annual gross revenues above $26,625,000 (an amount adjusted every two years), or buying, selling or sharing the personal information of 100,000 or more California consumers or households each year. Whether or not these laws apply to Estimea, we honor the following requests from every user in the United States:

  • Access and portability: export of your estimates in JSON format, through “Export my estimates” in Settings; to know what other personal information we hold about you, write to us.
  • Correction of your data.
  • Deletion: the “Delete my account and my data” button erases your account, your estimates, your photos, your shared pages, your reports and your referral. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in Google Play, otherwise you will keep being charged.
  • Opting out of personalized advertising, and of any “sale”, “sharing” or “targeted advertising” within the meaning of state laws: through “Advertising choices” in Settings, shown to residents of the states whose law provides for this choice, or, wherever you live, by deleting your phone's advertising ID in its settings (Google › Ads, or Privacy › Ads, depending on the model). Estimea is an app: it does not receive the “Do Not Track” signals that some web browsers send.
  • Objection, restriction and withdrawal of your consent, at any time, by writing to contactdexdevapp@gmail.com.

To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm a request from the email address linked to it; an authorized agent can also make a request on your behalf. If we decline a request, we tell you why, and you can appeal by replying to our answer: we respond to your appeal within 45 days and, if we maintain our refusal, tell you how to contact the attorney general of your state. Exercising your rights never changes the price or the quality of what you receive.

6. COMPLAINTS

If you think your rights are not being respected, you can first complain to us at contactdexdevapp@gmail.com. You can also report a problem to the Federal Trade Commission (reportfraud.ftc.gov) or to the attorney general of your state; in California, to the California Privacy Protection Agency (cppa.ca.gov). As the publisher is established in France, you can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés), the French data protection authority, at www.cnil.fr.
